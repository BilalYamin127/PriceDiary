PriceDiary Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how PriceDiary – Personal Price Tracker handles information. “We” refers to the PriceDiary development team. PriceDiary is an offline application for recording and comparing prices you enter yourself.

1. Information you enter

You may enter product names, brands, categories, units, quantities, shop names, prices, currencies, dates and notes. PriceDiary also saves record identifiers, entry timestamps and your currency and theme preferences. Avoid placing sensitive personal information in notes unless you intend to store it on your device.

2. Local storage and developer access

PriceDiary stores your diary in local application storage using Hive CE. The app does not operate an application backend and does not send your diary to us. We cannot view or recover your local records. The app does not require an account, and it does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services that send usage data to the developer.

The current app does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera or microphone. Selecting a backup file uses your device's file picker; sharing an export uses the operating system's sharing interface.

3. Backups, exports and sharing

You can choose to export CSV files or save a full JSON backup. These files can include all products, shops, prices, dates, notes and settings in your diary. The files are not encrypted by PriceDiary. You control whether and where to save or share them. If you select email, cloud storage or another application, that destination receives the selected file and handles it under its own policies. PriceDiary does not automatically upload exports to a developer-controlled server.

Your operating system or device provider may back up application data according to your device and account settings. Such backups are handled by that provider rather than by a PriceDiary backend. Review those settings if you do not want device backups. A web version, if used, stores data in browser storage; clearing browser data can remove it.

4. Retention, restoration and deletion

Records remain locally until you delete them, clear app storage, uninstall the app or otherwise remove the relevant storage. You can delete individual observations and unlinked products or shops. Settings includes a Clear all diary data option. Restoring a backup replaces the active diary after confirmation.

Deleting data inside PriceDiary does not delete copies you previously exported, shared or stored in operating-system backups. Remove those copies separately through the destination or backup provider. Uninstall and backup behavior can vary by device; we cannot guarantee recovery after deletion.

5. Security

The diary database and exported files are not encrypted by the application. Device access controls and any device-level encryption are provided by your platform. Use a device lock, choose private export destinations and keep backups in a location you trust. No storage or transfer method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Purchases, advertising and third parties

The current version contains no ads, payment processing or in-app purchases. Libraries used for interface rendering, navigation, local storage, charts, file selection and sharing support app functionality. We do not use them to send your diary or usage information to the developer. External destinations that you select for sharing may process the exported data under their own terms.

7. Children

PriceDiary is a general-purpose price diary and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer backend. Parents and guardians should supervise device use and sharing where appropriate. This statement does not replace the publisher's target-audience and content-rating declarations in an app store.

8. Privacy requests and contact

Because we do not receive your diary, requests to access, correct or delete local diary data should be carried out in the app or through your device settings. For questions about this policy, contact the PriceDiary development team at privacy@example.invalid.

Publication note: privacy@example.invalid is a placeholder and is not a monitored address. The publisher must replace it with a working public contact address before publishing this policy or the app listing.

If you contact the publisher using a real contact address after publication, the publisher receives the information you choose to include in that message. Do not attach your diary or other sensitive information unless necessary for your request.

9. Changes to this policy

The publisher may update this policy when app behavior changes. The current policy should be made available at the public privacy-policy link in the app listing. The effective date above identifies this version. Material changes should be reflected in the policy and applicable app-store disclosures.
